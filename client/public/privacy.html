TeamHub — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

TeamHub is an internal team-workspace application (chat, tasks, workflows, calls, client management and payroll access) operated by KNAP Advisory (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data the TeamHub website and Android app collect, why, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written to reflect how the app actually works.

TeamHub is used by member firms and their staff. Your employer/workspace administrator is the data controller for your workspace’s content; we act as the processor that runs the software. This policy covers the app itself.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you (or your admin) create an account, we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed password, plus your role, job title and avatar colour/photo if you add one.

Workspace content

Content you create in the app — messages, tasks, comments, workflows, client records, notes, deadlines and files you upload — is stored so the workspace functions. This is visible to members of your workspace according to their permissions.

Camera & microphone

Used only during audio/video calls you start or join. Call media is streamed peer-to-peer between participants; we do not record calls.

Location — optional, opt-in only

Location is off by default. It is collected only if you explicitly turn on “Share my location” in Settings → Location. When on:

  • Only your current (latest) position is stored — we do not keep a history or movement trail.
  • It is visible only to the administrators of your own workspace, for on-duty coordination (e.g. field visits).
  • The app displays a persistent on-screen banner the entire time sharing is active, so it is never hidden from you.
  • Location is captured only while the app is open and sharing is on — there is no background/hidden tracking.
  • You can turn it off at any time; your stored position is deleted immediately when you do.

Notifications & device data

If you enable push notifications, we store a push token to deliver them. We also process basic technical data (e.g. app version, and your IP address as part of normal internet requests) to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the workspace — chat, tasks, files, calls and related features.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and workspace secure.
  • To deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • To show opted-in location to your workspace admins for on-duty coordination.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service.

We process this data to deliver the service you and your employer have signed up for, and on the basis of your consent where the law requires it (for example, location and notifications).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We share personal data only with:

  • Members of your own workspace, per your permissions (this is how the app works).
  • Infrastructure providers that host the service and deliver push notifications, acting on our instructions.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Location is shared only within your workspace’s admin view, and only while you have sharing on.

5. Retention

Account and workspace content is retained while your account is active and as your employer requires. Your latest shared location is kept only while sharing is on and is deleted when you turn it off or when your account is removed.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Turn location sharing and notifications on or off at any time in the app.
  • Access or correct your profile information in Settings.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us (below). Your workspace admin can also deactivate/delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. location) at any time.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

TeamHub is a workplace tool intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

9. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Changes
