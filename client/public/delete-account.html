TeamHub — Delete your account & data

You can request deletion of your TeamHub account and the personal data associated with it. This page lets you submit that request; you don't need to be signed in.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, title, avatar) and login credentials.
  • Your last shared location, if any (this is deleted immediately whenever you turn location sharing off).
  • Your notification tokens and personal preferences.

What may be retained

Content you created inside a workspace — messages, tasks, comments and files — may be retained by your organisation for its business and legal records, and where retention is required by law. Where retained, it is disassociated from your profile where feasible.

Request deletion

Enter the email address on your TeamHub account. We verify and action requests within 30 days, and email you when it's done. Your workspace admin can also delete your account directly from within the app.

Prefer email? Write to knapadvisory@gmail.com from your account address. See also our Privacy Policy.
